01. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Creating a warm and approachable coffee brand.

Brew Haven Coffee is a self-initiated concept project created to practice developing social media content for a fictional coffee shop brand.

The project focused on creating a cohesive visual identity across different types of social media content, including brand awareness, product promotion, lifestyle content, and student-focused promotions.

Industry Coffee / Food & Beverage
Project Type Self-Initiated Concept
Role Creative VA / Graphic Designer
Deliverables 5 Social Media Posts

02. PROJECT GOAL

Every design has a purpose.

The goal was to create social media content that would make Brew Haven feel warm, welcoming, modern, and approachable.

Introduce the Brew Haven visual identity.

02

Highlight products and promotional offers.

03

Connect with college students.

04

Lifestyle Content

Create emotional and relatable coffee moments.

03. CREATIVE DIRECTION

Warm, cozy, modern.

Brand Personality

Cozy · Modern · Friendly · Approachable

Visual Style

Warm coffee tones, cream backgrounds, natural lighting, lifestyle photography, and bold typography.

Target Audience

Students, young adults, coffee lovers, and people looking for a comfortable place to relax or study.

06. WHAT I LEARNED

More than just making things look good.

This self-initiated project helped me practice working from a client-style brief, developing a consistent visual identity, creating different types of social media content, and refining designs based on feedback.

I also learned that effective social media design is not only about making something visually appealing, but also about communicating a clear purpose to the audience.
